# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Next.js (App Router) and TypeScript, self-contained, production started from the standalone server. The brief left the stack open and asked for a self-contained site with no CMS, database, CRM, or third-party form service.

## Users

Homeowners in South West London (and nearby Surrey) considering a loft conversion, extension, kitchen, bathroom, interior, staircase, or whole-house refurbishment. They need to judge the work, understand that one team designs and builds, and request an estimate or a survey.

The site is also a live demo for the two directors. Jason Hunter (co-director, former modelmaker and film set designer) judges photography, composition, typography, and motion. Leon Morrell (co-director, carpenter and joiner) judges whether joinery, staircases, and detail are shown properly.

## Product Purpose

A marketing website for Encompass London that shows real project photography, explains the one-team design-and-build offer, and lets a visitor get an instant loft guide price and send an enquiry. Success for the demo: both directors feel the work has been shown properly, and the brand matches the logo. Success for a visitor: they understand the offer, trust the proof, and can request an estimate or call.

## Positioning

One team designs and builds. Architectural drawings, planning or permitted development, structural engineering, building control, and the build itself sit under one roof, with one point of contact. Loft conversions trade as Encompass Lofts.

## Operating Context

Shown on a laptop and on a phone, including in person to the directors. Office visits are to 338 Garratt Lane, Earlsfield, London SW18 4EL. Phone 020 8871 3323. Email jason@encompasslondon.com. Forms, including the estimator, stay on the page and are not submitted anywhere.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Multi-page marketing site: home, loft conversions, extensions, kitchens, bathrooms, interiors and refurbishments, staircases and joinery, filterable projects with story pages, area pages, about, contact, instant loft estimator.
- No CMS, database, CRM, or third-party form or booking service. Nothing is submitted to a server.
- Estimator logic, prices, and build times are fixed by the brief. The result is a guide price. A survey is required for a fixed quote. Completion copy states a director will call within 1 working day to arrange a free survey. That call does not happen from this demo; the thank-you state is front-end only.
- Do not invent figures, awards, testimonials, opening hours, or project facts that were not supplied. Review cards stay marked as placeholders until real Houzz wording is pasted in.
- Logo sits on black only.
- Palette is the logo palette only. No yellow or amber.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Encompass London. Loft conversions trade as Encompass Lofts. Footer copyright: Encompass London LLP.
- Logo: black ground, dotted fan or compass in metallic gold grading from bronze to light gold. Primary file from encompasslofts.com; alternative on Houzz. Never on white or cream.
- Palette, given exactly: ink black #0A0A0A, panels #141414 and #1C1C1C, gold #E0A030, bronze #B06030, light gold #F0D090, metallic gold gradient linear-gradient(135deg, #B06030 0%, #E0A030 45%, #F0D090 70%, #E0A030 100%), text #F3EFE6, muted #A8A196. At most one warm off-white #F3EFE6 section per page.
- Type: refined high-contrast serif for headings (Didone, in the spirit of Cormorant or Playfair); clean geometric or grotesk sans for body and UI; wide-tracked uppercase for labels.
- Feel: architecture monograph or luxury property magazine. Photography leads. Full-bleed images, asymmetric grids, thin gold hairlines, slow motion (Ken Burns, clip-path reveal, subtle parallax, gold underline draw, button sheen), with prefers-reduced-motion respected.
- Voice: confident, concise UK English. No filler.

## Product Principles

- Show the work at the scale the photography deserves. Jason should recognise his frames; Leon should see the joinery.
- The brand follows the logo: black and metallic gold, never the old yellow.
- One team, one point of contact. Say that plainly and do not pad it.
- Every figure on the site is either supplied or absent. Placeholders stay labelled.
- The phone and a short form are always within reach. On a phone, the number is tappable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast, including gold on black for small text (use light gold where gold fails). Keyboard access for the lightbox, menu, and estimator. Visible gold focus. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Mobile-first, including tap-to-call.
