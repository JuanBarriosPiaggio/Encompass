# Home

Mode: Persuade

Audience: homeowners in South West London, and directors Jason Hunter and Leon Morrell judging a live demo.
Job: show the photography properly, prove one team designs and builds, and start an estimate.
Action: Get an instant estimate, or call the studio.
Proof: real Houzz project photographs, 4.9 from 40 reviews, Best of Houzz years supplied in the brief.
Constraints: pinned palette and logo rules, no invented testimonials or figures, front-end forms only, one light section.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An architecture monograph for a design-and-build studio. The page refuses the builder template (icon grids, yellow brand, equal cards, stock photography) and lets full-bleed project photographs carry the offer.

OWN-WORLD: Ink black ground, near-black panels, one cream reviews section. Metallic gold is a hairline, a gradient border, a price, and a sheen, never a fill. Didone display at poster scale, grotesk for UI, wide-tracked light-gold labels. Sharp corners. Photography flush to the edge. Solid black plates hold type so it never relies on a scrim.

STORY: The visitor sees the rooms first, understands that drawing and building are one team, checks a live loft guide price, and asks for a survey. Jason should recognise the frames. Leon should find the staircases.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed cross-fading photograph with a slow Ken Burns drift. A solid black plate at the lower left carries the label “Earlsfield · South West London”, the serif headline “Lofts and extensions, designed and built by one team.”, a gold-bordered “Get an instant estimate” and a ghost “View our work”. The header is transparent, logo on black, and turns solid with a gold hairline on scroll.

FORM: Brief-pinned. The user specified the palette, the Didone-and-grotesk pairing, the motion, and this first viewport. Composition was not reopened.
seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
